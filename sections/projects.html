04

"Nochmal" - Adaptive German Audio Learning System*

German audio can sound like a blur even when you know the words on paper.
I just started building an adaptive listening app where learners first hear a short German passage, unpack it sentence by sentence, and then listen to the exact same recording again to see what changed.
Under the hood, it combines controlled LLM lesson generation (700+ lessons from A1-B1), German voice models, structured grammar breakdowns, and a learner model that decides what each person should hear and review next.
Work in progress - the long-term goal is to test whether that learning carries over to German the learner has never heard before.

05

Signalz - Sales Hiring Intelligence

Built at a GTM hackathon, the product scans public job postings for early sales-growth signals, such as a company hiring both a sales manager and individual contributor or a founding sales/GTM hire.
n8n orchestrates Apify searches, Featherless LLM groups jobs by company and explains “why now.”
A live run processed 20 jobs, identified 15 companies and surfaced 8 qualified signals.
Placed third in the ACT Track category.
